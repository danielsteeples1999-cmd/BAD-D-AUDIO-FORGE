# AUDIO FORGE EXPERIMENT PROTOCOL

Every experiment separates:
1. Fixture truth
2. Direct measurement
3. Interpretation
4. Evidence level
5. Human listening evidence

Required record: experiment ID, question, hypothesis, fixture, baseline, change, measurements, attacks, regression, limitations, reusable capability, next action.

Audio rules:
- Do not claim decoded PCM equality from container bytes.
- Never use tolerance to hide an unexpected audio difference.
- Browser playback claims require browser/runtime evidence.
- Physical-device claims require device evidence.
